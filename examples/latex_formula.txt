Let
$$
U=\{u_0,\ldots,u_{m-1}\},\qquad V=\{v_0,\ldots,v_{m-1}\}.
$$
The graph $G$ is defined as follows:
$$
V(G)=T\cup \{p,q\}\cup \{w_0,\ldots,w_{m-2}\}.
$$

The edge set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
E(G)=
&\ \{\{u_i,p\}\mid 0\le i\le m-1\}
 \cup \{\{v_i,q\}\mid 0\le i\le m-1\}
 \cup \{\{p,q\}\} \\
&\ \cup \{\{u_{i+1},w_i\},\{v_i,w_i\}\mid 0\le i\le m-2\} \\
&\ \cup \{\{w_i,w_{i+1}\}\mid 0\le i\le m-3\} \\
&\ \cup \{\{u_0,w_0\},\{w_{m-2},v_{m-1}\}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
